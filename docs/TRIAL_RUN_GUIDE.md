# MITRA NIDHI CHITI PRO Trial Run Guide

## Start Point

Open the dashboard and use:

```text
Dashboard -> Quick Actions -> Start Trial Run
```

The checklist opens inside the dashboard. Every action button routes to an existing page.

## Sample Trial Chit Data

Use this sample data for the trial:

```text
Chit Name: Trial Chit July 2026
Chit Value: ₹1,00,000
Members: 10
Monthly Payment: ₹10,000
Duration: 10 Months
Start Month: July 2026
```

## Browser Trial Steps

1. Click `Start Trial Run` on the dashboard.
2. Open `Create Chit Group` and create the trial chit group.
3. Open `Add Members` and add 10 members.
4. Open `Activate Group` and activate the group after details are complete.
5. Open `Collect Month 1 Payments` and collect full payments for sample members.
6. Open `Test Partial Payment` and enter one partial payment.
7. Open `Test Pending Member` and confirm the pending member appears.
8. Open `Test Duplicate Payment Protection` and attempt the same member/month again.
9. Open `Run Auction / Lucky Draw` and create the first auction/draw.
10. Open `Declare Winner` and declare only one winner.
11. Open `Generate Receipt` and generate a receipt for the collection.
12. Open `Check Member Ledger` and verify member payment history.
13. Open `Check Pending Report` and verify pending amount.
14. Open `Check Finance Update` and verify cash, bank, income, and profit movement.
15. Return to `Check Dashboard KPIs` and verify dashboard KPIs, lifecycle, business health, AI insights, reports, and finance summary.

## Status Meaning

- `Pending` means the prerequisite data is not visible yet.
- `In Progress` means the prerequisite exists and the step is ready to perform.
- `Completed` means the app can detect matching data for that step.

## Safety

- No new pages are required.
- No PowerShell commands are required for the trial.
- All checklist buttons route only to existing MITRA NIDHI CHITI PRO pages.
