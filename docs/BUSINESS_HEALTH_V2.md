# Business Health V2

`BusinessHealthV2Engine` calculates score from repository-shaped source data.

## Metrics

Collection discipline, pending risk, cash flow, payout readiness, profitability, expense control, member retention, reconciliation, data quality, operational compliance, investor exposure, and growth readiness.

## Output

Overall score, status, metrics, warnings, recommendations, explanations, and data timestamp.

## Known Limits

The scoring weights are deterministic local rules. They should be reviewed with real trial data before production grading.
